About S L I E T
Established in 1989, Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology 
(SLIET) embarked on a journey towards technical excellence since it’s 
inception. Institute is accredited with Grade A by NAAC. The institute awards 
its own Certificates, Diplomas, Undergraduate (NBA accredited) and 
Postgraduate degrees (approved by AlCTE, New Delhi) as per multi-entry and 
multi-exit policy of NEP-2020. The institute has a sprawling area of 451 acres 
surrounded by lush green land. The campus presents a spectacle of harmony 
and natural beauty. SLIET provides an extensive platform through several 
clubs and societies for the personality development & upskilling of students.

ELECTRONICA
Circuit Craft
A two-stage electronics competition beginning with a timed quiz covering analog 
electronics, digital logic, and circuit theory. Qualified participants proceed to a 
practical circuit-design and debugging challenge using either physical 
breadboards or approved circuit simulation software. Participants are judged on 
circuit functionality, accuracy, debugging skills, neatness, and time efficiency.
Digital Design Challenge
An individual HDL competition where participants solve digital design problems 
involving combinational logic, sequential circuits, FSMs, memory interfaces, and basic 
processor components. Participants can use only Verilog or VHDL and must complete 
the problems within a fixed time limit. Solutions are evaluated for functionality, 
efficiency, readability, and proper HDL design.
Innov a tion-X
A 10–12 day innovation sprint where teams identify a real-world problem and develop a 
functional MVP using hardware, software, IoT, AI/ML, web, mobile, or hybrid 
technologies. Teams are expected to build a working prototype, maintain their source 
code, and prepare technical documentation and a pitch. The event concludes with a 10-
minute presentation followed by a 5-minute Q&A with the judging panel.
Arduino Imagino
An embedded-systems challenge where participants develop an Arduino-based solution 
to a student-related real-world problem provided 1–2 weeks before the event. 
Participants will work with sensors, embedded programming, and realtime feedback 
mechanisms to create their solution. Physical hardware or approved simulation software 
may be used depending on the event setup.
